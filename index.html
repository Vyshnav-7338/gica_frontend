06 / FAQ

Good questions.
Clear answers.

What is GICA?+

GICA stands for Global Influencers & Creators Association, a professional global community connecting creators and creator-economy stakeholders.

Who can join GICA?+

Influencers, content creators, digital creators, emerging creators, creative professionals, brands, agencies and eligible organizations.

Does GICA connect creators with brands?+

Yes. The ecosystem is designed to facilitate relevant connections and collaboration among creators, brands, agencies and industry partners.

What programs does GICA support?+

Creator meetups, workshops, conferences, masterclasses, networking programs, development initiatives and recognition opportunities.
